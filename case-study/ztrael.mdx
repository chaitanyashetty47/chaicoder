## The Challenge

Ztraél had strong interest in its products but lacked a proper online commerce experience.

Customers primarily discovered products through Instagram and social channels.

Orders were handled manually through:

* WhatsApp conversations
* Instagram DMs
* Direct customer inquiries

As demand increased, this process became difficult to manage and scale.

The existing website functioned more like a digital brochure than an online store.

Customers could browse products, but purchasing required direct communication with the team.

Ztraél didn't need more traffic.

**They needed a system that could turn product interest into a structured buying experience.**

---

## The Solution

We rebuilt the entire experience using **Lovable + Shopify**.

The goal was to launch quickly without sacrificing quality.

### Phase 1 - Planning & Store Architecture

* Brand positioning refinement
* Product page copy generation
* Landing page structure
* Customer journey mapping

### Phase 2 - Shopify Implementation

* Shopify integration setup
* Product catalog creation
* Individual product pages
* Inventory management configuration
* Checkout flow implementation

### Phase 3 - Launch & Optimization

* Product variants and bundles
* Mobile responsiveness
* Content and copy refinement
* Blog setup
* Final QA and launch

The first working version was delivered within 48 hours, with the complete store launched within 7 days.

---

## Project Outcome

* Transformed a brochure-style website into a fully functional Shopify store
* Eliminated manual order handling through WhatsApp and DMs
* Created a structured customer purchase journey
* Enabled self-service inventory and order management
* Delivered a responsive eCommerce experience across devices
* Established scalable infrastructure for future growth

Ztraél moved from manually managing orders to operating through a dedicated commerce platform.

---

## Why Shopify

The client needed a solution they could manage independently after launch.

Shopify enabled:

* Product management without developer involvement
* Inventory tracking
* Order processing
* Customer checkout workflows
* Operational simplicity for a small team

This reduced ongoing technical overhead while creating a foundation for future expansion.

Ztraél didn't need a more beautiful website.

They needed a commerce system.

By combining Lovable, Shopify, and a streamlined implementation process, we transformed a manual sales operation into a scalable online store in just 7 days.

**The outcome wasn't just a redesign - it was operational transformation.**
